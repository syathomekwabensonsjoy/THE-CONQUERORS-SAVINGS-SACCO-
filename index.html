About

THE CONQUERORS SAVINGS SACCO is a family-based savings group that promotes financial discipline, cooperation, and mutual support among members to help them grow their savings and build a stable financial future together.

Mission

To promote disciplined saving, cooperation, and shared financial growth among family members for a stable and successful future.

Services

Member savings accounts, deposits, withdrawals, financial records, and savings monitoring.

Terms and Conditions – The Conquerors Savings SACCO

Membership in The Conquerors Savings SACCO requires commitment to regular saving and responsible participation. Every member is expected to contribute agreed savings within the set timelines to promote the growth and sustainability of the SACCO. All deposits, withdrawals, and other transactions must be properly recorded and confirmed by the SACCO administrator. Members must provide accurate personal information during registration. Members are responsible for protecting their account login details. Any misuse of the SACCO platform may lead to suspension or termination. The SACCO leadership reserves the right to update these terms when necessary to improve service delivery and financial management.
